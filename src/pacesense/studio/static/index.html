        How PaceSense works

        PaceSense predicts a marathon finish from runners like her: for each of her recent races it finds similar runners who ran that time and looks at what they ran next (case-based reasoning, as in Muijlwijk et al., IUI ’24).

        Weights say how much each race counts. Move a slider, or just say why a race is unusual:

        • “2016 was a hot day and she was injured”
        • “trust the recent races more”
        • “ignore Berlin” · “Amsterdam was her best day” · “undo”
        • “I think she can run 3:25” (records your own estimate)
        • “simulate 26 degrees with a 15 km/h headwind and she starts too fast”

        Every change gets a card that says whether her own times agree. In the demo her fastest race is hidden, so you can see whether your changes move the prediction closer to what really happened; press Reveal at the end.
